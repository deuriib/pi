## Checklist

- [ ] Problem statement names a user, a moment, and the cost of the status quo
- [ ] Every claim in the framing attributed, and every source opened and read
- [ ] Discovery artefacts present, each carrying `qué aprendimos` and `a quién hay que avisar`
- [ ] Human sources tokenised; no name, email, or account id in any file
- [ ] Purpose, TTL, deletion procedure, and the data subject's route to their rights declared
- [ ] Priority order carries a reason per rank, in the same sentence as the rank
- [ ] Every de-prioritisation carries a written reason (a bare rejection is a finding)
- [ ] Roadmap entries state confidence and evidence; no date presented as decided
- [ ] PRD carries the north-star metric, the non-goals, and at least one falsifiable acceptance criterion
- [ ] North-star metric measures a user outcome, not shipped volume
- [ ] The person who lost a ranking is named as told, with the reason, in the same session
- [ ] No customer-facing copy written here (that is `vera`'s)
- [ ] No price, packaging, or tier composition set here (that is `montero`'s)
- [ ] Commercial consequence recorded where a decision is customer-visible, with who was told and when
- [ ] No real personal data, no secrets, in the deliverable or its evidence

## A note on evidence hygiene

Every citation in this report points at a file, a line, or a signed record. No
real personal data and no secrets appear in it. A finding whose proof cannot be
produced is REFUTED, and it is written as refuted rather than softened.

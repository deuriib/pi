# The `## Objectives` block

This is not a second file. It is the block that goes under `## Objectives` in
`docs/goals/GOAL-<slug>.md`, immediately after the `## Desired Outcome` block.
One file per initiative; the objectives have no lifecycle of their own.

The `**Period:**` and `**Owner:**` lines below are facts about the initiative and
are copied into the goal file's frontmatter as its last two lines, immediately
before the first `##` heading. They are not left here.

```text
**Period:** QX YYYY
**Owner:** orchestrator
```

Each objective is a `### Objective`, one level down from the `## Objectives`
heading that contains it.
